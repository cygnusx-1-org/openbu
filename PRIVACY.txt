Privacy Policy for Openbu

Last updated: 2026-05-13

Openbu ("the app") is an open source Android application that lets you
monitor and control Bambu Lab 3D printers running in Developer Mode /
LAN-only mode. This document explains what data the app handles and
how.

The short version: Openbu does not collect, transmit, sell, or share
any personal information. All communication happens directly between
your device and your printer (or a relay you choose to run).


WHO RUNS OPENBU

Openbu is developed and maintained by the project's open source
contributors. The source code is available at:

    https://github.com/cygnusx-1-org/openbu

For privacy questions, please open an issue at:

    https://github.com/cygnusx-1-org/openbu/issues


WHAT DATA THE APP HANDLES

Data you provide

To connect to a printer you provide:

  - Printer IP address (often auto-detected on your local network)
  - Printer serial number (often auto-detected on your local network)
  - LAN access code
  - Optional RTSP URLs for external camera streams
  - Optional filament type and color assignments per AMS tray and the
    external spool

This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private
storage. It is used solely to connect to the printer you configured.
It is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party.

Data the app reads from your printer

While connected, the app reads operational data published by the
printer over MQTT, FTPS, and the camera stream -- for example: nozzle
and bed temperatures, fan speeds, AMS state, print job progress, HMS
error codes, and the camera image. This data stays on your device. It
is displayed to you and is not sent anywhere else by the app.

Data the app does NOT collect

Openbu does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting SDKs,
tracking, fingerprinting, or any other telemetry. The app does not
create user accounts and does not require sign-in. The developer
receives no information about you, your printer, or your usage of the
app.


NETWORK COMMUNICATION

The app communicates over the network only with destinations you
configure or that it discovers on your local network:

  - Your Bambu Lab printer over MQTT (TLS, port 8883), the MJPEG
    camera stream (port 6000), FTPS for file management, and/or RTSP
    for models that expose an RTSP stream.
  - mDNS / multicast on your local network in order to auto-detect
    printers.
  - An RTSP server if you have added an external RTSP URL.
  - An openbu-relay instance if you have configured remote access.
    openbu-relay is self-hosted software -- when you use it, you (or
    whoever operates that relay) control where the traffic goes. The
    Openbu project does not operate any relay or backend service on
    your behalf.

The app does not contact the developer's servers, Bambu Lab's cloud,
or any analytics or advertising services.


ANDROID PERMISSIONS

The app requests the following permissions, all for the functionality
described above:

  - INTERNET -- to communicate with the printer (and your relay, if
    configured).
  - ACCESS_WIFI_STATE and CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE -- to discover
    printers on the local network via mDNS.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photos,
microphone, call logs, SMS, or other sensitive data.


CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any
information from anyone, including children.


CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the updated version will be committed to the
project's Git repository alongside the source code. The "Last updated"
date at the top of this document will be revised.
